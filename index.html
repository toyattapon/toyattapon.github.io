About Me

Beyond finding bugs

“I believe good QA is not just about finding bugs. It's about understanding how a system works, identifying risk, improving feedback loops, and helping teams deliver software with confidence.”

I work best in environments that value autonomy, clear ownership, and engineering-focused collaboration. I enjoy solving problems independently while collaborating closely with developers, product teams, and stakeholders when needed.

My goal is not to compete with AI, but to become a better engineer by using AI as a force multiplier — a productivity tool, not a replacement for QA thinking.
